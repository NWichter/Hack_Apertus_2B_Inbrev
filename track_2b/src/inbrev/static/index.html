Load a demo inbox and press Sort inbox.

Inbrev routes by handling path and administrative deadline. It never assesses clinical content.

Triage mode (default): administrative sorting with a safety net. Messages that may contain symptoms leave automation and go to a person, without any ranking.
Clinical research mode (INBREV_MODE=clinical-triage, off by default): adds a keyword-based urgency hint to flagged messages. Research prototype, not a medical device, not validated.
