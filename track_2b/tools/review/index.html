    How it works

    Pass 1 covers every message. In blind mode (default) the gold label is hidden and the form starts empty. In check mode the gold label is shown and prefilled; confirm it or correct it. Each save appends one decision to the log; the latest decision per message and pass counts.

    Pass 2 is a blind second labelling of 60 messages (seed 2026; all gold safety_flag=true messages if there are at most 60, the rest drawn at random) in a shuffled order. Neither gold nor your pass-1 labels are shown. Start it at least 48 hours after pass 1, so that you do not remember your answers.

    Statistics gives Cohen's κ with a 95% confidence interval (κ ± 1.96·SE, SE = √(po(1−po) / (n(1−pe)²))) for pass 1 vs pass 2 (intra-rater) and for pass 1 vs gold. kappa.py computes the same numbers from the exported log.
