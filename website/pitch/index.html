Purposeful use of AI · model choice

Why Apertus, and what it costs.

Measured, same pipeline

  • Qwen3.5-9B, Ministral 8B: 177/180 routes

Why Apertus anyway

  • Only openly available training data, opt-outs applied, personal data filtered
  • Published data catalogue and EU training-content summary
  • Local run and quote checks work with any model: no argument

Intended purpose · Implementation feasibility

Administrative by default. Clinical mode is research only.

Inbrev does

  • Sort by path and deadline
  • Extract fields, each with its quote
  • Say “you decide” when unsure

Inbrev never does

  • Rank by clinical severity
  • Assess symptoms or suggest care
  • Send patient data out (local mode)
Clinical research mode: off by default, not validated, not certified (MDCG 2019-11, MepV Art. 15).
